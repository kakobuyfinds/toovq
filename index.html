02 / A clearer way to browse

From a find to a considered choice.

01

Find the exact listing

Use the OopBuy spreadsheet selection to shortlist an item, then open its CAFANS detail page. Record the original product link, selected color, size and listing date so you can compare the same option later.

02

Confirm before paying

Review the seller’s measurements, current price and available variants. Where CAFANS provides an OopBuy buying option, check the destination and confirm that the item can currently be purchased through your account.

03

Review photos and the total

If inspection photos are available, compare the received item with the option you ordered. Resolve visible issues and missing measurements before arranging onward shipping, then review the final shipping quote and applicable charges.

05 / About this spreadsheet

Useful context, alongside the catalog.

An OopBuy spreadsheet is a product-discovery list used to organize finds and source links for further research. A useful entry identifies an exact listing, its category and the information you still need before ordering.

This page presents an independent, browser-based selection from CAFANS. You can browse every product without an account or download. Search and sorting apply to the eight displayed items, rather than the entire CAFANS catalog.

How this selection is maintained

The names, images and estimated USD amounts were taken from CAFANS featured listings on November 19, 2026. The source links let you revisit each product instead of relying on a saved price alone. This page does not automatically synchronize with CAFANS.

For a personal shortlist, save the product link, exact variant, original currency, measurement notes and date checked. Keep seller statements separate from details you have confirmed. Revisit the source before making a purchase.

06 / Quick answers

OopBuy spreadsheet FAQ.

What is this OopBuy spreadsheet?

It is an independent product-discovery guide with a selected CAFANS catalog, estimated USD prices, category filters and comparison advice. It is a webpage rather than a Google Sheets document.

Where do the OopBuy spreadsheet and Buy it now buttons go?

Both buttons open https://cafans.com. Individual product cards open their corresponding CAFANS detail pages. You can review the listing and any available shopping-agent options there.

Are these real product listings?

Yes. The eight displayed names, images, prices and source links were taken from the CAFANS featured catalog on November 19, 2026. That confirms their source, not their current stock, authenticity or purchasing eligibility.

Are the displayed prices the final checkout amounts?

No. They are estimated USD amounts displayed by CAFANS at the time of review. Selected variants, currency conversion, delivery, service charges and applicable taxes can change the total. Confirm current amounts before paying.

Can I order every displayed product through OopBuy?

Eligibility has not been confirmed for every item. Open the CAFANS detail page, review any OopBuy option and check the current listing and purchasing conditions in your account before placing an order.

How should I choose a size?

Compare the listing’s measurement chart with a garment or pair of shoes that fits you. Keep the units and measurement method consistent. If a needed measurement is missing, ask for it rather than relying only on a size label.

Does this page provide QC photos or guaranteed delivery times?

No. The displayed images are source catalog photographs. Obtain any available inspection photos through the purchasing service you choose, and check delivery estimates for your destination and route.

Is this the official OopBuy website?

No. This is an independent information page. It is not operated by OopBuy, does not process orders or payments, and does not provide warehouse or shipping services.
